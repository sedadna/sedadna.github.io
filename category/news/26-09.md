<h4 class="section-title underline">New Positions</h4> 

<br>
<p align="justify">A <a href="https://employment.ku.dk/all-vacancies/?show=167653" target="_blank">Staff Scientist or Research Consultant in Bioinformatics</a> position is available at the Centre of Ancient Environmental Genomics at the Globe Institute, University of Copenhagen. The position is 3 years fixed-term where the candidate's role will include processing and analysing large-scale sequencing data, and designing and automating robust pipelines for data summarisation, visualisation, and quality control.</p>
<p align="justify">Application deadline is the <b>15th September 2026</b>. If you'd like more information, please contact <a href="mailto:tskorneliussen@sund.ku.dk"><b>Thorfinn Sand Korneliussen</b></a> or <a href="mailto:mwpedersen@sund.ku.dk"><b>Mikkel Winther Pedersen</b>.
<br>
<p align="justify">A different<a href="https://candidate.hr-manager.net/ApplicationInit.aspx?cid=3010&ProjectId=167656&DepartmentId=19996&MediaId=4918" target="_blank"> Staff Scientist or Research Consultant in Bioinformatics and Genomic Reference Catalogue Development at Globe Institute </a> position is also available at the Centre of Ancient Environmental Genomics and AEGIS, University of Copenhagen. This position is 4 years fixed-term, where the candidate will play a central role in developing large-scale genomic reference catalogues that capture the genomic diversity of life on Earth. The primary objective of the position is to identify, acquire, curate, and continuously expand collections of genomes and other relevant FASTA resources from public repositories, scientific initiatives, and other openly available sources worldwide.</p>
<p align="justify">Application deadline is also the <b>15th September 2026</b>. If you'd like more information, please contact <a href="mailto:tskorneliussen@sund.ku.dk"><b>Thorfinn Sand Korneliussen</b></a>.
