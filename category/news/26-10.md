<h4 class="section-title underline">New papers</h4>
<p><a href="https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Jazmin-Ramos-Madrigal" target="_blank"><b>Jazmín Ramos Madrigal</b></a><i> et al.,</i> (2026) published "<a href="https://royalsocietypublishing.org/rsos/article/13/9/260217/483229" target="_blank"><u><i>Ancient DNA preservation in the Caribbean</i></u></a>" in Royal Society Open Science.</p>

<p><a href="https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Monisha-Balasubramaniyan-2" target="_blank"><b>Monisha Balasubramaniyan</b></a><i> et al.,</i> (2026) published "<a href="https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10482-026-02433-4" target="_blank"><u><i>SPredicted shifts in microbial functional potential across the late glacial to holocene transition: a 16S rRNA-based inference from southeastern Arabian Sea sediments</i></u></a>" in Antonie van Leeuwenhoek.</p>

<p><a href="https://xaalioreilly.com/" target="_blank"><b>Xaali O'Reilly-Berkeley</b></a><i> et al.,</i> (2026) published "<a href="https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/jqs.70101" target="_blank"><u><i>Holocene vegetation dynamics of Grande Terre (Kerguelen Islands) inferred from sedimentary ancient DNA</i></u></a>" in the Journal of Quaternary Science.</p>

<p><a href="https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Tyler-Murchie/research" target="_blank"><b>Tyler J. Murchie</b></a><i> et al.,</i> (2026) published "<a href="https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/mec.70529" target="_blank"><u><i>Sedimentary Ancient DNA Tracks Multi-Kingdom Ecosystem Reorganizations Following Sequential Human Land Use at Crawford Lake</i></u></a>" in Molecular Ecology.</p>

<p><b>Yu Feng</b> <i> et al.,</i> (2026) published "<a href="https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1470160X26010022" target="_blank"><u><i>Differential responses of algal ecosystems in freshwater and Saline Lakes on the northeastern Tibetan plateau to climate and anthropogenic disturbances</i></u></a>" in Ecological Indicators.</p>

<p><a href="https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Kadir-Oezdogan/research" target="_blank"><b>Kadir Toykan Özdoğan</b></a><i> et al.,</i> (2026) published "<a href="https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/14614103.2026.2715207" target="_blank"><u><i>Towards an Archaeological Workflow for SedaDNA Sample Collection: Methods and Best Practices for Minimising Surface Contamination</i></u></a>" in The Journal of Human Palaeoecology.</p>

<p><b>Erika Ferreira Rodrigues</b> <i> et al.,</i> (2026) published "<a href="https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s44218-026-00155-8#Sec21" target="_blank"><u><i>Field sampling guidelines for sedimentary ancient DNA (sedaDNA) research in lacustrine and coastal systems: from field collection to laboratory subsampling</i></u></a>" in Anthropocene Coasts.</p>

<p><a href="https://www.researchgate.net/scientific-contributions/Nihan-Dilsad-Dagtas-2040053167" target="_blank"><b>Nihan Dilşad Dağtaş</b></a><i> et al.,</i> (2026) published "<a href="https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/edn3.70356" target="_blank"><u><i>Evaluating Rapid Extraction Methods for Recovering Ancient DNA From Archaeological Sediments</i></u></a>" in Environmental DNA.</p>
